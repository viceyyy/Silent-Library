Your Privacy Is Important To Us!

We may collect basic information such as your name, contact details, library account information, and website usage data.

Some library services and e-resources may be provided by third-party vendors. Their privacy policies may also apply when you use their services.

We do not sell your information. We only share information when necessary to provide services, comply with the law, or protect the library and its users.

You may request to access, correct or update your personal information. You can also contact the library with questions about how your information is used.
